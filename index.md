## Overview

Recent years have seen a paradigm shift in hypothesis testing and uncertainty quantification through the development of **$e$-values**. Defined as non-negative random variables with an expectation under the null hypothesis bounded by $1$, $e$-values provide an elegant framework for anytime-valid inference, sequential testing, and safe post-selection analysis.

This workshop at **NeurIPS 2026 (Paris Satellite)** brings together researchers in machine learning, statistics, game-theoretic probability, and sequential analysis to explore theoretical breakthroughs and emerging practical applications of $e$-values in modern ML systems.

## Call for Papers

We invite short paper submissions (up to 4 pages excluding references/appendices) on theoretical, algorithmic, or practical aspects of $e$-values and related topics, including but not limited to:

* **Foundations:** $e$-values, $e$-processes, and game-theoretic probability.
* **Multiple Testing:** False Discovery Rate (FDR) and Family-Wise Error Rate (FWER) control via $e$-values.
* **Sequential ML:** Anytime-valid inference, confidence sequences, and bandit algorithms.
* **Safe Inference:** Post-selection inference, conditional testing, and off-policy evaluation.
* **Applications:** A/B testing, LLM evaluation, trustworthy AI, and distribution shift detection.

### Submission Guidelines
* Papers must be formatted using the standard **NeurIPS LaTeX template**.
* Submissions are double-blind and must be anonymized.
* Accepted papers will be presented as posters or contributed oral presentations (non-archival).
